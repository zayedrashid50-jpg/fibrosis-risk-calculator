What moves this estimate

Effect of each item on the log-odds of stiffness ≥ 8.0 kPa, compared with a reference adult (45-year-old man, waist 98 cm, BMI 28.5, no diabetes, AST 20, ALT 20, GGT 22, platelets 240, albumin 4.2). Red raises risk, green lowers it.

About. A logistic regression model using 10 routine clinical and laboratory items, developed from US national survey data.

Limits. This tool helps guide the need for elastography; it is not a substitute for elastography. Derived from US adults; performance elsewhere is unknown. The model predicts FibroScan stiffness, which can read high in obesity, after meals and with active hepatitis.
